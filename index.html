FAQ

Questions

Do I need an account to see a trip or a plan?

No. A trip link or a plan link opens in any browser. You need an account to post trips, make plans, like, comment, save or follow.

What if a photo has no location?

Eonroamio asks you to tag the place and time for that photo.

Who can see my trips?

You choose for each route: everyone, your followers, or friends only. Please note: a shared link is visible to everyone.

Is Eonroamio free?

Yes. Eonroamio is always free to use, on iPhone and on the web. That includes planning with AI.

How does AI planning use my note?

We use AI to find the places in your note, work out how to get from one place to the next, and estimate how much time to spend at each one.

How do I delete my account?

Tap your avatar, then Settings → Help & Support → Delete account. Or email support@eonroam.io from the email address you registered with.
