How it works

A photo with no location? You tag its place and time.

What's in a trip

More than pins on a map.

Photos on the map

Every photo is pinned to the spot where it was taken, so the map shows where each moment happened.

Flights

Add a flight number. The leg shows the airline, the departure and arrival times, and whether it landed on time.

Trains

Add where the train started and ended, and when it left and arrived. The leg is drawn as a train track.

Likes and comments

React to a photo, a stop, a flight, a train, or the whole route.

Save for later

Save a photo, stop, flight, train or a whole route to come back to.

Follow people

Follow others, and let others follow you. When you follow each other, you become friends.

You can choose to approve each follow request first.
FAQ

Questions

Do I need an account to see a trip?

No. A shared link opens in any browser. You need an account to post trips, like, comment, save or follow.

What if a photo has no location?

Eonroamio asks you to tag the place and time for that photo.

Who can see my trips?

You choose for each route: everyone, your followers, or friends only. Please note: a shared link is visible to everyone.

Is Eonroamio free?

Yes. Eonroamio is always free to use, on iPhone and on the web.

How do I delete my account?

Tap your avatar, then Settings → Help & Support → Delete account. Or email hello@eonroam.io from the email address you registered with.
